# Changelog

## 2026-11-12

### Material

- Allow explicit, narrowly scoped, user-granted standing permission under section 4.7, including recurring automated replies within defined limits.
- Require verification of the grant and its continued validity, exact scope checks, immediate observance of changes and revocation, and an audit trail. Prohibit agents from creating, inferring, expanding, or renewing permission themselves.
- Clarify in sections 6.4 and 6.5 that valid standing permission satisfies 4.7, may support authorized work while the user is unavailable, and does not override other hard rules or separate authorization requirements.
- Synchronize the canonical and operative documents and update the README summary.
